## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for modern Indian small and medium business operators who require high-velocity operational clarity, seamless bilingual adaptability (English and Hindi), and robust multi-device reliability. The aesthetic fuses enterprise-grade reliability with contemporary digital lightness, eliminating unnecessary decorative noise while ensuring distinct operational status readability across fast-paced trading, manufacturing, services, and distribution contexts.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Minimalism with Pragmatic Warmth
The visual language operates on three fundamental pillars:
- **Pragmatic Efficiency:** High signal-to-noise ratio where financial figures, customer follow-up cadences, and lead states are legible at a glance under bright ambient shop conditions or high-density office monitors.
- **Dignified Trust:** Anchored by deep indigo and disciplined navy surfaces that convey financial stability and institutional competence without feeling bureaucratic.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** Native baseline alignment, balanced vertical metrics, and equal visual weight between Latin scripts and Devanagari numerals and glyphs.

## Colors

The palette uses a crisp, high-contrast light system designed for rapid visual parsing and rigorous data status distinction:

### Primary & Brand Authority
- **Primary Navy (`#1E3A8A`):** Core structural accent, primary brand containers, navigation rails, and dominant page titles.
- **Interactive Blue (`#1D4ED8`):** Primary interactive targets, focused rings, active tab indicators, and primary command triggers.
- **Subtle Navy Tint (`#EFF6FF`):** Selected table rows, active menu state backgrounds, and primary hover fills.

### Functional Status System
- **Teal / Won & Positive (`#0D9488` / `#14B8A6`):** Closed-won opportunities, received payments, verified phone and GSTIN checks, and positive metrics. Paired with a soft background (`#F0FDFA`).
- **Amber / Due Today & Pending (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`):** Immediate attention items, today's callbacks, and pending approvals. Paired with soft background (`#FEF3C7`).
- **Crimson / Overdue & Risk (`#EF4444` / `#DC2626`):** Overdue collections, cold drop-offs, and critical system alerts. Paired with soft background (`#FEF2F2`).

### Surfaces & Neutral Typography
- **App Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Primary base background for dashboards, avoiding harsh monitor glare.
- **Surface Elevation 0 / Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast content planes, data tables, and input envelopes.
- **Structural Border (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise 1px boundaries defining card containers, table cell partitions, and input borders.
- **Subtle Divider (`#F1F5F9`):** Secondary rules inside dense lists and metadata clusters.
- **Text Headings (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate charcoal for primary labels, metric counts, and titles.
- **Text Body & Secondary (`#334155`):** High legibility secondary copy for lead company names and bilingual subtitles.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`):** Timestamps, helper labels, and placeholder states.

## Typography

The design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typographic applications. Its geometry offers wide open apertures, generous x-height, and precise glyph architecture that retains extreme legibility in Latin characters, international currency glyphs (₹), and dense numerical ledgers.

### Bilingual Handling (English & Devanagari)
- Ensure all line heights reserve a minimum `1.4` multiplier ratio to accommodate Hindi vowel signs (matras), conjuncts, and ascenders/descenders without clipping.
- Never use font weights below `400` for Hindi text; light weights degrade rapidly across affordable Android device screens with lower pixel densities.
- Numeric displays utilize tabular lining numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure Indian numbering formatting (Lakhs and Crores e.g., ₹12,50,000) columns align strictly along decimal points.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an 8pt base grid with a 4pt micro-adjustment scale for tight data clusters:

### Grid Architecture & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `2rem` outer page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Collapsible 240px sidebar navigation with fixed utility header.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Navigation contracts to a slim 64px icon rail.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `0.75rem` gutters. Standardized persistent bottom tab navigation for thumb-reach actions.

### Mobile Ergonomics & Touch Compliance
- All interactive touch items (inputs, floating action buttons, list anchors) maintain a strict minimum bounding target of `48px × 48px`.
- High-frequency phone and WhatsApp shortcut triggers are prioritized within the bottom 40% vertical screen zone on handheld displays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is primarily driven through disciplined border containment and clean surface layering rather than intense, dark drop shadows. This avoids visual fatigue in high-density CRM environments.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` background bound by a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and a delicate ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive States):** Elevated border with subtle transition to `#CBD5E1` and ambient shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdown Menus & Popovers):** Pure white container layered with: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Sheet Drawers):** Backdrop overlay `#0F172A` at `40% opacity`, card shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The system uses a balanced geometric structure (`level 2`) to deliver approachable, modern SaaS ergonomics without sacrificing enterprise precision:

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius.
- **Cards and Module Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) corner radius.
- **Modals, Floating Action Trays, and Bottom Sheets (`rounded-xl`):** `1rem` (16px) top and side radius.
- **Badges and Avatar Tags:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) for clear categorical separation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1E3A8A` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius. Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: `#1E40AF`. Focus-visible ring: `2px solid #3B82F6` with `2px` offset.
- **Positive / Won Action:** Solid `#0D9488` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover: `#0F766E`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, text `#DC2626`. Hover: background `#FEE2E2`.
- **Touch Standard:** Height is 40px on desktop (`padding: 0 16px`) and 48px on mobile (`padding: 0 20px`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- Compact pill-shaped containers with vertical alignment and `0.75rem` line-height text (`label-sm`).
- **Won / Active:** Background `#F0FDFA`, text `#0D9488`, border `1px solid #99F6E4`.
- **Due Today / Follow Up:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Overdue / Critical:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#DC2626`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.
- **Neutral / New Lead:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Cards & Information Tiles
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Internal padding: `1.5rem` desktop, `1rem` mobile.
- Section header within cards features a bold title (`headline-sm`) accompanied by direct operational action (e.g., "Add Lead", "Call", "WhatsApp").

### Input Fields & Search
- Border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`.
- Height: 42px on desktop, 48px on mobile for finger accuracy.
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Focused state: border `#1D4ED8`, outline ring `3px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`.
- Support prefix icons (Lucide icons e.g., `Search`, `Phone`, `IndianRupee`) aligned 12px from the left edge.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Minimum hit target `44px × 44px` enclosing the visible 18px control.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#1E3A8A`, icon `#FFFFFF`, border `#1E3A8A`.

### Lists & Data Grids
- Alternating or bordered list rows with `1px solid #F1F5F9` row dividers.
- Hover state: `#F8FAFC`.
- Pinned customer contact columns with prominent click-to-call (`lucide-phone`) and WhatsApp (`lucide-message-circle`) inline triggers.
